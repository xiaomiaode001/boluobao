# Article social-series content map

## Source

Title: `允许AI先想一会，打开了另一条增长曲线`

## Thesis lock

Allowing a model to spend computation while answering gives it room to decompose, check, retry, and verify; this creates a capability-scaling direction after training, with especially stable feedback in domains such as code and mathematics where outcomes can be verified.

## Factual and narrative locks

- The opening contrast is immediate answer generation versus working through a hard problem with time and scratch space.
- The reasoning process may decompose the task, inspect intermediate results, find an error, backtrack, and try again before producing the answer.
- The model is not portrayed as suddenly receiving a different brain; the change is the space available for thought and verification.
- Capability can increase with both training-stage computation and answer-stage thinking computation.
- Verifiable outcomes provide clearer reinforcement feedback in code and mathematics than open-ended preference alone.
- Do not invent benchmark values, equations, company logos, named researchers, products, interfaces, or stronger claims about all language tasks.

## Frame map

### Frame 1 — Entry tension

Role: show the brittleness of the shortest problem-to-answer route.

Proposition: a small thinking machine rushes down one narrow route; the first joint is wrong and pieces fall before inspection, while paper and pencil remain unused.

### Frame 2 — Mechanism

Role: show decomposition, inspection, backtracking, and verification.

Proposition: the same machine follows a loop through three unequal work stations, compares the middle state, erases one route, and assembles a coherent blank output.

### Frame 3 — Consequence

Role: show an additional capability-growth direction after training.

Proposition: after a completed lower scaffold, a second path continues upward; fitting results remain in the climb and failed results fall away, with time and scratch work supporting the extension.

## Series anchors

- Recurring motion cue: burnt-orange route changing from straight, to looping, to rising.
- Shared format: landscape `16:9`, warm ivory paper, dark-brown ink, muted colored pencil, one verified source-aligned phrase per frame.
- Progression: brittle speed → reflective loop → continued growth.

# AI classroom platform-cover content map

## Exact copy

- Chinese: `AI走进课堂以后，我们到底希望学生学会什么？`
- English: `When AI Enters the Classroom, What Do We Really Want Students to Learn?`
- Each language appears exactly once per cover. No other text.

## Narrative lock

The cover asks an open education question after AI enters the classroom. It must not answer the question, claim that AI replaces teachers or students, or invent a learning outcome.

## Shared motif

One student writes on an unfinished sheet while a small neutral AI answer box offers blank answer cards. The sheet branches toward three subordinate possibilities: a magnifying glass, an incomplete bridge, and a seedling. These stand for inquiry, construction, and growth without labeling or turning the cover into a literal infographic.

## Platform layouts

- WeChat Official Account: broad `2.35:1` title/hero split; Chinese and English occupy the left quiet field; student and AI interaction counterweight the right; three possibilities sit at the far right as one loose route.
- Xiaohongshu: portrait `3:4`; Chinese title first, English immediately below, then student/AI interaction and branching sheet descend through the page for mobile scanning.
- X: user-locked `5:2`; title block on the left, student/AI interaction in the middle, and three possibilities on the right; all elements remain large enough for feed viewing.

## Exclusions

No logos, handles, dates, category tags, classroom slogans, UI chrome, statistics, body copy, pseudo-writing, copied reference text, photorealism, vector polish, or identical crop reused across platforms.

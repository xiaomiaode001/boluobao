# Visual Subject Quality Gates

Read this file with the base [quality rubric](quality-rubric.md) for food, scenes, landscapes, landmarks, and people.

## Single-food gate

A single-food result targets **19/20** only when:

1. exactly one food presentation is present; its required vessel or wrapper is part of that presentation;
2. requested item count and diagnostic features are correct without relying on a label;
3. the hero occupies 55–70% of the canvas with generous paper and no identity-critical crop;
4. text is limited to one name/title and at most one short observation unless more is requested;
5. one cast-shadow stain and at most one sourced physical accident are present, neither reading as an extra object part;
6. at least three construction-error families remain visible outside protected semantic zones;
7. subject fidelity, silhouette simplification, line character, physical-media feel, annotation integration, and exclusions each score `2`;
8. there are no unrelated foods, drinks, utensils, props, pseudo-writing, invented decorations, unexplained blocks, floating seams, or attached shadow artifacts.

## Scene gate

A scene targets **18–19/20** only when:

1. the place or event is readable without annotations;
2. one hero anchor dominates, only two or three middle anchors compete, and the atmosphere remains incomplete;
3. near, middle, and far layers fall off in contour weight, interior detail, and color coverage; one support object stays partial and one major plane stays open;
4. major spatial contours remain mostly continuous and repeated architecture or texture does not become stitched or uniformly outlined;
5. one dominant ground, floor, wall, path, shore, or horizon direction holds the space while only selected secondary planes disagree;
6. raw paper functions as light, air, wall, sky, glare, or ground rather than surviving only at the border;
7. figures and vehicles have ground contact, remain diagrammatic, and do not read as isolated stickers;
8. no unrequested signage, pseudo-writing, logos, license plates, posters, floating geometry, or protected-area artifacts appear.

## Landscape gate

A landscape targets **19/20** only when:

1. dominant landform, weather or light, path or water direction, and one scale cue read without annotations;
2. exactly three depth bands are present: a cropped foreground interruption, an imperfect middle route, and a background mass;
3. the main route, shoreline, river edge, and horizon remain continuous without periodic dashed, beaded, or stitched contours;
4. no more than two medium-to-strong pencil masses dominate, with raw paper active inside natural surfaces;
5. repeated trees, waves, grass, rocks, and leaves are reduced to representative groups;
6. at least four uneven spatial or material error families remain visible;
7. there is no notebook binding, postcard border, centered scenic symmetry, invented signage, extra narrative prop, photorealistic atmosphere, or protected-area artifact.

## Landmark gate

A landmark targets **19/20** only when:

1. outer silhouette, critical tier or mass count, orientation, required colors, and 2–4 signature features remain correct without the title;
2. line character and shape/perspective naivety each score `2` while identity-critical geometry stays readable;
3. 60–80% of repeated secondary windows, tiles, stairs, rails, balusters, paving, or decoration is omitted or merged;
4. at least four error families appear in different safe zones rather than one repeated wobble;
5. raw paper remains visible through sky, terraces, paving, or facade bands and centered postcard symmetry is avoided;
6. notes remain subordinate, with no copied reference text, invented signage, or unexplained marks.

## Character-portrait gate

A character portrait targets **19/20** only when:

1. head and jaw shape, skin tone, head angle, expression, face-framing hair or accessories, 2–4 facial relationships, clothing layers, pose, and signature accessory remain recognizable;
2. identity-critical face contours are continuous and sparse, with no random mark, fill accident, pseudo-writing, scar, or correction crossing a protected feature;
3. person count, arm and hand count, wrist ownership, overlap order, contact, and gesture meaning remain coherent;
4. one hero person uses at most one dominant role prop and two incomplete context fragments;
5. hero outline is stronger than face, hands, garment, and prop interiors, with no stitched perimeter or modeled skin;
6. dark garments avoid edge-to-edge equal-density hatching and crisp paper-colored pseudo-graphics;
7. at least three error families occur outside face and hand zones;
8. warm paper remains active, palette stays within 4–7 earthy colors, and there is no photorealism, anime, chibi, polished comic finish, copied text, logo, watermark, or extra person.

## Close-up-character gate

A chest-up or waist-up person targets **19/20** only when:

1. identity or persona skeleton, restrained expression, gesture, hand count, wrist ownership, and prop contact remain readable;
2. the person occupies roughly 58–70% of the page, one low-information edge is cropped asymmetrically, and 30–40% active paper survives;
3. head, hair, jaw, and body silhouette remain continuous and heavier than diagnostic facial, finger, garment, and prop marks;
4. the person is the only hero, with one dominant role prop and at most two incomplete context fragments;
5. faces and hands remain protected while at least three error families occur on hair, sleeves, furniture, props, shadows, fills, or background;
6. large hair and clothing masses retain directional grain and paper exposure without texture carpet or pseudo-graphics;
7. line character, silhouette simplification, physical-media feel, and exclusions each score `2`;
8. there is no extra person, complete room inventory, logo, watermark, copied wording, pseudo-writing, or invented garment or device symbol.
